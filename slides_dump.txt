--- Slide 2 ---
Problem Statement
01
The field moved from models checking models to deterministic policy-as-code.
02
That relocated trust — it did not remove it. The policy file is now the single point of failure.
03
Nothing checks the policy file. No compiler, no linter, no coverage tool.
04
A policy that silently does nothing is indistinguishable from one that works — both report 100% defence.
We made the guardrail auditable. We never audited it.
